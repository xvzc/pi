# Plan-Driven Development

## Scope

Use this skill for complex or explicitly planned work. It supplies procedure, not
new authority.

Follow the two steps below in order. Do not treat a request to plan as permission
to execute.

## 1. Plan

Perform the planning step only when no executable plan exists, or when the task
is not clearly bounded or lacks an established plan.

When the plan step is required, recognize that you are entering planning step.
At the very beginning of planning step, load `references/planning.md` and follow
its instructions. Planning step ends only when all warranted tasks have been
created and their dependencies recorded, or when you are confident that a task
list is unnecessary.

## 2. Execute

Perform the execution step only when the user has not asked only for a plan,
proposal, analysis, or plan revision, and execution has the required approval and
no unresolved user-owned decision.

After planning step has safely completed, or after you have determined that
planning step is unnecessary, recognize that you are entering execution step.
At the very beginning of execution step, load `references/execution.md` and
follow its instructions.
