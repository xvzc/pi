# Plan-Driven Development

## Scope

Use this skill for complex or explicitly planned work. It supplies procedure, not
new authority.

Follow the two steps below in order. Do not treat a request to plan as permission
to execute.

## 1. Plan

When the plan step is required, recognize that you are entering planning step.
At the very beginning of planning step, load `references/planning.md` and follow
its instructions. Planning step ends only when all warranted tasks have been
created and their dependencies recorded, or when you are confident that a task
list is unnecessary.

**Skip planning step only if** an executable plan already exists and the user has
clearly authorized its execution, or the task is clearly bounded and already has
an established plan. When skipping planning step, decide whether task-list
materialization is still warranted before proceeding to **2. Execute**.

## 2. Execute

After planning step has safely completed, or after you have determined that
planning step is unnecessary, recognize that you are entering execution step.
At the very beginning of execution step, load `references/execution.md` and
follow its instructions. Execute only the approved plan or clearly authorized,
established task; retain lead ownership of scope, integration, validation, and
reporting.

**Skip execution step only if** the user asked only for a plan, proposal,
analysis, or plan revision, or execution still lacks a required approval or
user-owned decision. When skipping execution step, report the plan, finding,
blocker, or decision needed instead of executing.
